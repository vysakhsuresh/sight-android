Ikshana — four marks

At 200, 96 and 48 px, and on a light shelf. Each is one idea, filled and lit, made to be seen from across a room.

A · Bindu — the point that sees

No eye at all. A single warm point of light in deep indigo, and the rings of seeing spreading from it. The bindu is where sight begins; it also looks like a pupil catching light, a sonar ping, a lens flare.

B · Padmaksha — the lotus eye

The old Indian word for a beautiful eye is "lotus-eyed". Two lotus petals in coral and gold make the lids; the iris is a deep teal disc with a light in it. Warm, Indian, and no other camera app looks like it.

C · Dawn aperture — the camera that lights the dark

A camera aperture, but what shows through the blades is a sunrise: amber light rising into a night-blue sky. The app opens a shutter on a dark world and there is light behind it. Reads as camera at 48 px, as warmth at 200.

D · The guiding hand

An open palm, and above it a point of light with its halo. Ikshana's second meaning is looking after; this is the hand that is offered, and the light it holds up. Nothing about cameras, everything about care. The one a blind user would choose.
